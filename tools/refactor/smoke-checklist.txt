Manual smoke list for the refactor (about 10 minutes). Do it on the build of the commit being checked and note anything that differs from
the build of the tag pre-refactor. The headless checks (tools/refactor/check.py) cover logic; this covers what is drawn.

Start: build\dw3mv.exe, with the data folder set. Nothing is saved to the game in any step unless it says so.

 1. Files        The model list loads; click a Digimon: it opens in the view; the motion bar shows under the view.
 2. Menus        File / Edit / View / Help open; View > Browser panel and Inspector panel hide and show (Ctrl+B / Ctrl+I); F1 opens the controls.
 3. Model        Bones list: the eye hides a bone, a click selects it, its name shows over it; "Selected bone" fills in the right panel.
 4. Motions      Pick another motion: it plays; Space pauses; the arrow keys step; the speed slider changes the rate.
 5. Fighter      WarGreymon: the scripts list is grouped; "Play the script" on script 11 plays it (motions, cameras, effects).
 6. Fighter (A/B)  Candidate camera: pick a donor (Agumon) and a camera, tick "Use the candidate"/B: the framing changes, the pose does not;
                 A / B and key B switch, also while it plays; Fit to this model / Undo the fit; Save as preset, then click it in the list.
 7. Fighter (Choreography)  Donor WarGreymon script 11, target MetalGarurumon script 11 (open MetalGarurumon first): Build and play;
                 open a shot, Override, Reset the shot; Save as application, Duplicate (right-click), Delete (confirm).
 8. Cameras list  In the right panel: Free camera, a Battle view, a Portrait, a Battle shot each look through that camera.
 9. Effects      Original > Common: an effect plays; the effect bar (tick slider) works; the mods tab lists the mods' effects.
10. Textures     The texture pages list; a TIM shows with its palette; click a polygon: its bone is selected.
11. Stages       Stage 1 opens; click a part: it is selected; Ctrl+drag moves it; Ctrl+Z undoes it (do not save).
12. DW2          The DW2 tab opens its folder section without errors (a DW2 folder is optional).
13. Quit         Close the window: no "unsaved" prompt unless something was edited.

Write what you see for each (ok / differs / n.a.). Anything that differs is a regression of the step, not a thing to fix inside it.
